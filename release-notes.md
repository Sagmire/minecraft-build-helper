## Minecraft Build Helper 1.1.0

A fresh new look ✨

- Pixel-style headings and a strip of grass and dirt along the top, for more of a Minecraft feel.
- A welcome banner on Learn with a little 3D cottage, built and drawn by the app.
- Chunky buttons you can "press", cards that lift when you point at them, and clearer
  highlights for the tab you're on (especially on phones).
- Fixes: an Undo message no longer lingers after you leave a plan or switch tabs, and the
  "Add photos" button is easier to read.
