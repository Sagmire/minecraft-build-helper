## Minecraft Build Helper 1.0.0

The first version! 🎉

- **Learn**: 13 building tips, each with a common mistake next to a better way.
- **Palettes**: block sets by style, favourites, "start from a block" and texture gradients.
- **Inspiration**: paste, drag in or add photos and sort them into categories.
- **Planner**: sketch builds floor by floor and get a list of the blocks you need.
- **Checklist**: review a finished build and see which tips to look at next.
