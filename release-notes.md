## Minecraft Build Helper 1.0.0

The first version! 🎉

- **Learn**: 13 building tips, each with a common mistake next to a better way.
- **Palettes**: block sets by style, favourites, "start from a block" and texture gradients.
- **Inspiration**: paste, drag in or add photos and sort them into categories.
- **Planner**: a simple plan for deciding what to build, and a 3D block plan for placing every
  block layer by layer, with a 3D view you can turn around, shape tools, mirror, a hotbar and a
  list of the blocks you need. Rulers show how many blocks across, deep and high things are,
  and terrain mode lets you add the ground, water and trees that are already there.
- **Checklist**: review a finished build and see which tips to look at next.
- **First-time setup** picks tips for your experience and favourite palettes for the styles you
  like, then offers a **quick tour** of the app. Both can be run again from ⚙️ Settings.
- The installer lets you choose where the app is installed.
